## Three seconds everyone will replay

The reveal itself is over in a moment: the pop, the burst of colour, the gasp, the hug. Everything around it is the build-up, and the build-up is where a gender reveal picnic in Toronto earns its place. Friends and family gathered around a beautiful setup, a backdrop behind you, every phone already recording.

We deliver, set up and style the whole thing in a park, on a beach, in a backyard, at home or in an indoor venue across the GTA. When the cheering has settled and the photos are taken, we come back, pack everything away and clean up.

## Choosing how to reveal

The booking form asks for the parents' names, the due date or month, and how you'd like to reveal: a balloon box or balloon pop, a cake cut, confetti, smoke, or “Not sure yet” if you'd like to talk it over.

One thing worth knowing before you choose: some parks don't allow smoke or confetti. If the spot you have in mind is one of them, we'll suggest a park-friendly reveal that still gives you the moment you're picturing. Add the park name or area in the form so we can take it into account when we reply.

If even you don't know the result yet, turn on the surprise section and tell us who's holding the secret.

## The setup behind the moment

We recommend our Celebration package for a reveal. It's a full themed setup with a backdrop, a balloon garland or flower arch, custom signage and games for the wait, starting at $1,200 for 6–8 guests for 3 hours, before HST. Inviting more people than that? We'll quote your group. Prices vary by location across the GTA, and we confirm your exact quote by message.

Want both a balloon garland and a flower arch? Add whichever one isn't in your setup. A custom cake, a neon sign and a photographer are optional too. Tick them in the form and we'll include them in your quote.

## Countdown to the reveal

1. **Tell us the plan.** The parents' names, due date or month, your reveal of choice, a date, a backup date and the area.
2. **Hear from us within 24 hours.** We confirm availability and your quote, and suggest a park-friendly reveal if your spot needs one. A $100 deposit, or 50% for larger events, holds the date.
3. **Three, two, one.** Everything is styled when your guests arrive, and we take care of the clean-up afterwards.
