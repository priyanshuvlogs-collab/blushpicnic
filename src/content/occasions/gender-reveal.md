## Three seconds everyone will replay

The reveal itself is over in a moment: the pop, the burst of colour, the gasp, the hug. Everything around it is the build-up, and the build-up is where a gender reveal picnic in Toronto earns its place. Friends and family gathered around a beautiful setup, a backdrop behind you, every phone already recording.

We deliver, set up and style the whole thing in a park, on a beach, in a backyard, at home or in an indoor venue across the GTA. When the cheering has settled and the photos are taken, we come back, pack everything away and clean up.

## Choosing how to reveal

The booking form asks for the parents' names, the due date or month, and how you'd like to reveal: a balloon box or balloon pop, a cake cut, confetti, smoke, or “Not sure yet” if you'd like to talk it over. The reveal is your choice; if you'd like help sourcing something for it, ask and we'll tell you what's possible.

One thing worth knowing before you choose: confetti, glitter and party poppers aren't permitted in public parks, and some parks don't allow smoke. If you're planning a park reveal, we'll suggest a park-friendly one that still gives you the moment you're picturing. Add the park name or area in the form so we can take it into account when we reply, and see [our confetti policy](/policies#confetti) for the details.

If even you don't know the result yet, turn on the surprise section and tell us who's holding the secret.
