## Off-site, without the off-site planning

If you're the person who books the team events, you know the routine. Pick a spot, order the decorations, arrive early to set up, then stay behind to pack it all away while everyone else heads home. A corporate picnic in Toronto takes care of everything after the first step.

You choose the date and the spot: a park within walking distance of the office, a beach for a summer social, or an indoor venue anywhere in the GTA. We deliver and style a full themed setup before your team arrives, then come back to clear everything away when you're finished. Your only job is getting people away from their inboxes on time.
