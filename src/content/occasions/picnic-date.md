## The kind of date you don't have to organise

Most dates come with homework. Book the table, find parking, hope the patio isn't too loud to talk on. A picnic date in Toronto can feel completely different, as long as somebody else does the setting up.

That's where we come in. You choose the spot and the time. We arrive first with a low table, rugs and cushions, real tableware and glassware, fresh florals and a letter board that says whatever you'd like it to. There's a Bluetooth speaker for your playlist and a few games for when the conversation wants a break. When your two hours are up, we come back, pack everything away and clean up, so the date ends with the two of you, not with folding rugs.

It works for a third date, when you want to make an impression. It works just as well in year eight, when you want it to feel like a third date again.

## Where it could happen

A picnic date suits almost anywhere you can picture a low table: a park you both love, a quiet spot by the lake, your own backyard, or your living room if you'd rather stay in. We set up in parks, on beaches, in backyards, at home and in indoor venues across Toronto and the GTA. Prices vary by location, so tell us the park or neighbourhood when you book. If you haven't decided, choose “Not sure yet” and we'll suggest a spot.

Food isn't part of the package, which means dinner can be anything: a takeout order from the place you always go, a homemade spread, or a grazing board or custom cake added to your booking.
