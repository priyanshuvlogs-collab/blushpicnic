Not every marriage starts in a ballroom. If you said your vows at city hall, in a backyard or with just two witnesses, a picnic gives a small wedding a celebration of its own, in a park, on a beach, at home or in an indoor venue anywhere in the GTA.

Had the big wedding already? Book it for your first week, or first month, of married life: a reset for the two of you before ordinary routines return. Or let the families join afterwards.
