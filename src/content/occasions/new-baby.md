## One visit instead of twenty

When a baby arrives, so do the visitors. The doorbell rings, the kettle goes on, someone tidies before and after, and the same story gets told six times in a week. It's lovely, and it's a lot.

A welcome baby picnic in Toronto gathers everyone in one place at one time. Some families call it a sip and see: an easygoing afternoon for grandparents, aunts, uncles and close friends to meet the newest member of the family, with the setting up and clearing away taken off your hands. We deliver, set up and style the picnic, then come back to pack it away and clean up when you're done.
