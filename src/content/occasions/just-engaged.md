## The question has been asked. Now for the fun part.

The proposal happened, the calls have been made, and somewhere in the group chat someone has already asked about the date. Before the wedding planning takes over, take an afternoon simply to be engaged. An engagement picnic in Toronto is a soft, beautiful pause: the two of you, or the people who cheered loudest, around a low table styled for the occasion.

No seating charts, no venue tours, no spreadsheets yet. Just a few hours of being a little giddy together.

Our Signature Picnic is made for it. You get a low table, rugs and cushions, tableware and glassware, fresh florals, a Bluetooth speaker, games and a letter board with your message. It starts at $375 for 2 guests for 2 hours, plus $35 per extra guest, before HST, and prices vary by location across the GTA. We deliver it, set it up and style it in the park, beach, backyard, home or indoor venue you choose, then pack it away afterwards.

## For the couple, or for the people who love them

**If you're the couple**, tell us both your names and your engagement date when you book, choose the spot and the colours, and arrive to it finished. The letter board is yours to fill: “Just engaged”, your new favourite word, or the date it happened.

**If you're the sister, best friend or parent** who wants to spoil them, mark it as a surprise and tell us how they'll arrive. Our team can stay out of sight nearby or leave before they get there; it's your call. You get the credit, and we won't mind one bit.

Either way, the extras are there if you want them: a photographer for announcement photos, a flower arch or neon sign as a backdrop, a custom cake or a grazing board to share. Each is optional and priced into your quote.

## How it comes together

1. **Share the details.** Both names, the date you'd like, a backup date and the area. About three minutes, start to finish.
2. **Get your quote within 24 hours.** We confirm availability, and a $100 deposit holds the date.
3. **Celebrate.** Arrive to everything set. When you're ready to head home, we take care of the clean-up.
