## One afternoon off the list

The months before a due date are full of lists: names to shortlist, a nursery to paint, a car seat to figure out. A baby shower is the afternoon when the parent-to-be gets to put the list down and simply be celebrated. Someone has to make that afternoon happen, and if you're reading this, it's probably you.

A baby shower picnic in Toronto takes the heaviest part off your plate. We deliver, set up and style everything in a park, on a beach, in a backyard, at home or in an indoor venue anywhere in the GTA. When the shower winds down, we come back to pack up and clean up. You get to enjoy the party you planned, rather than spend it running it.

## What we'll ask, and why

The booking form has a short section written for showers: the parents' names and the due date or month. Those details can find their way onto the custom signage, so the setup feels made for this baby rather than any baby. Then tell us the colours and vibe you have in mind, whether that's a soft neutral palette, a nod to the nursery, or a theme the parent-to-be has been hinting at for months.

Planning it as a surprise? Switch on the surprise section and tell us who else knows and how the guest of honour will arrive. Our team can stay out of sight nearby or be gone before anyone gets there.
