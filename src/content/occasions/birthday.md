## For whoever's organizing this year

Every birthday has a quiet hero: the friend, partner, sibling or parent who books, invites, reminds everyone and ends up carrying all the bags. If that's you, a birthday picnic in Toronto takes the heavy lifting off your list without taking the thoughtfulness out of it.

You tell us the essentials in the booking form: whose birthday it is, the age they're turning, and their favourite colours or characters. We take it from there, delivering, setting up and styling the picnic in the park, backyard, beach, home or indoor venue you choose, then packing it all away when the party's over. You get to arrive with everyone else and actually enjoy it.
