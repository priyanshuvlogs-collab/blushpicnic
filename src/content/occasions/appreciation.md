## Thanks that lands

A card passed around the office. A gift card sitting in an inbox. Thank-yous like these are kind, and forgotten by Friday. An appreciation picnic in Toronto is harder to forget. It's time set aside, with a styled table, fresh florals and a message with their name on it, that says “we noticed”.

For a team, it's a change of scene that doesn't feel like another meeting. For one person, whether that's a mentor, a parent, a nurse or the friend who got you through a hard year, it's the gift of an unhurried afternoon. And unlike a card, it's a thank-you the whole group gets to share, rather than sign and pass along.
