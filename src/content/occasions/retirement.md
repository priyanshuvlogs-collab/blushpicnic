## A farewell that isn't cake by the photocopier

Most retirements get the same send-off: a card that has done the rounds of every desk, a slab of cake beside the photocopier, a few words squeezed into the last half hour of a Friday. It's kind, and it's over in twenty minutes. After decades of early starts and showing up, the people who care about them usually want to give them something with a little more ceremony.

A retirement picnic in Toronto gives the day a setting of its own. Pick a park near the office, a beach they love, the family backyard or an indoor venue anywhere in the GTA. We deliver, set up and style everything before the guest of honour arrives. When the speeches and stories are done, we come back to pack up and clean up, so nobody ends the day loading décor into their car.

## Whether you're the colleague or the family

**If you're organising through work**, the booking form asks the questions you'd expect: who's being honoured, your company name, the message or years of service you'd like to mark, and whether you need an invoice. Add your headcount and the area, and we'll quote it.

**If you're family**, leave the company name blank and tell us about the person instead: their favourite colours, the nickname only the family uses, the grandchildren who'll be there. Turn on the surprise section if they don't know it's coming.

To round it off, add a custom cake, a grazing board to share, a neon sign, or a photographer to catch the speeches and the hugs. Tick them in the form and we'll price them into your quote.
